Design Notes

Career Cards
2-column grid. High-match cards get a subtle violet gradient wash in the corner and stronger border. Match badge uses intensity-coded colors. "Why it fits" section in amber adds personalization. Cards link to individual career detail + AI chat pages.
Card Hover
Subtle lift (translateY -2px) + deeper shadow. No heavy glow — keeps the focus on content. Border brightens slightly. On click, navigates to career detail page.
Auth Design
Centered glass card on star-field background. Same nebula + star treatment as other pages for consistency. Rounded inputs with violet focus borders. OAuth button uses subtle outlined style.
Auth Flow
Login, Sign Up, Forgot Password, and Update Password all share the same centered card layout. Only the form fields and titles change. Success states show a confirmation message in the same card frame.
Unauthenticated State
Careers page shows a CTA card instead of results: "Sign in to generate your personalized career matches" with login/signup buttons. Same card styling as career results for visual continuity.
Loading State
While generating recommendations, show skeleton cards with a subtle shimmer animation (gradient sweep left-to-right). Maintains layout stability and cosmic feel while AI processes.
