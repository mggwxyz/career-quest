What Changed

Language
Hero: "Find the Career You Were Made For" — direct, clear, no metaphor to decode. Subtext explains exactly what you'll do. Step titles use plain verbs: Pick, Answer, See.
Animated Stars
~50 procedurally placed stars with randomized twinkle animations (varying duration 3-8s, delays, max-opacity 0.3-0.8). A few accent-colored stars (violet, amber, cyan) add warmth. Subtle and atmospheric, not distracting.
Step Connectors
Removed the horizontal line that was overlapping the numbered circles. Replaced with small chevron arrows (→) between the cards — cleaner visual flow without artifacts.
CTA Language
"Begin Your Quest" → "Get Started" — lower friction, universally understood. "Learn More" → "How It Works" — tells you what you'll get.
