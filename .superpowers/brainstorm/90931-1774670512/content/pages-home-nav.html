Design Notes

Navigation
Floats 12px from top with rounded corners. Glass-morphism with subtle violet glow line on bottom edge. Collapses to hamburger on mobile with a slide-out drawer.
Hero Motion
On load: eyebrow fades in first, then headline staggers up word-by-word, subtitle fades in, CTA slides up. Stars twinkle with subtle CSS animation (opacity keyframes).
Steps Connection
Gradient line connects the three step numbers, shifting from violet → indigo → cyan. Each card staggers in on scroll with Framer Motion's viewport detection.
Responsive
Steps stack vertically on mobile. Nav collapses to logo + hamburger. Hero text scales down to ~36px. CTA buttons stack vertically.
